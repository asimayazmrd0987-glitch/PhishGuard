# Privacy and consent notes

Meeting Capture Studio is designed for intentional, user-started capture, not covert monitoring.

- The app requires a permission-confirmation checkbox before a session can start.
- The app keeps a visible recording status while capture is active.
- Recordings, screenshots, transcripts, and session metadata are saved to a local folder selected by the user.
- The app itself does not automatically send recordings to a server or upload them to cloud storage.
- Optional transcription processes audio locally, but the transcription library may download model weights on first use. That is a model download, not an upload of the meeting audio.
- Screen images can contain notifications, passwords, personal data, messages, and company information. Limit capture and access accordingly.

Before capturing a meeting, obtain appropriate participant notice and permission, and follow applicable laws and organizational rules. The app cannot determine what permission is legally required in a particular situation.
